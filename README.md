## 2. Structural Deficiencies of Legacy Forwarding Companies

1. **Dimensional Weight Pricing Penalties**:
   Traditional parcel forwarders frequently charge volumetric weight without repacking, inflating freight invoices by up to **250%** on lightweight bulky retail boxes. AQX Logistics applies precision repacking, discarding superfluous merchant packaging to cut billable volume by up to **70%**.
2. **Lack of Wholesale Cargo Pricing**:
   Competitors treat high-volume orders (auto parts, commercial spares, computer servers) under standard expensive consumer express rates ($14 - $22 / kg). AQX Logistics automatically unlocks the **$8.50 / kg** wholesale tier for consignments of 8.0 kg or more.
3. **Unexpected Destination Customs & DDU Duties**:
   AQX Logistics manages automated commercial customs invoices and DDP clearing protocols, removing post-arrival courier extortion at local customs gates.
